About me

A self-directed developer combining technical delivery with a long background in customer-facing and team-support work.

Since 2019, I've worked independently as a full-stack web developer, building roughly 100 web projects across social platforms, e-commerce, booking systems, dashboards, bespoke business applications and content-driven websites.

My work spans the full delivery cycle: understanding requirements, designing data structures, building features, testing changes, fixing regressions and deploying maintainable systems.

Before software development, I spent two decades in customer service, retail and team-support environments. That background remains useful: requirements are rarely perfectly tidy, communication matters, and the finished system has to solve the actual problem rather than just demonstrate the technology.
