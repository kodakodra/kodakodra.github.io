About Me

I'm a Senior Full-Stack Laravel Developer operating as KodaKodra. Since 2019 I've built web applications with a relentless focus on clean architecture, data integrity, and security. But more than that — I'm a problem-solver who thinks in systems.

My journey started long before code. Over 25 years in customer-facing roles taught me how to translate messy requirements into clear technical actions. Team leading and management (Level 3 qualified) gave me the discipline to own projects from concept to deployment without hand-holding.

I approach development like this: data first, then logic, then interface. I design normalised schemas that can answer tomorrow's questions, not just today's. Security isn't a checklist — it's woven into every migration, policy, and controller.

Outside code, I'm curious about how people and systems interact. I read, I build, I refine. I believe the best developers are the ones who never stop learning — and I intend to be one of them.

Core Beliefs

Code is communication

Write for the next person (or your future self). Clarity over cleverness.

Data informs everything

If you can't measure it, you can't improve it. Build with analytics in mind.

Security by design

Ownership & delivery
