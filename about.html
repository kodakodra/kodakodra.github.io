About me

A systems-minded developer with a background in operations, team leadership and technical delivery.

I'm a Senior Full-Stack Laravel Developer operating as KodaKodra. Since 2019 I've built web applications with a focus on clean architecture, data integrity and security.

My journey started long before code. More than 25 years in customer-facing roles taught me to translate messy requirements into clear technical actions. Team leading and management gave me the discipline to own projects from concept to deployment.

I approach development in a deliberate order: data first, then logic, then interface. I design schemas that can answer tomorrow's questions, and treat security as part of architecture rather than a final checklist.

Outside code, I'm interested in how people and systems interact. I read, build and refine continuously.
